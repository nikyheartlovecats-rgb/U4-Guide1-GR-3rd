Unit 4: Simple Past (Regular & Irregular Verbs)

Interactive Class Mode

📚 Grammar 1: Simple Past (Regular Verbs)

We use the Simple Past to talk about actions that happened and finished in the past. For regular verbs, we add -ed (or -d if the verb ends in e) to the base form of the verb for all subjects (I, you, he, she, it, we, they).

❓ Questions (Did + Subject + Base Verb)

  • Did you watch the fireworks?
  • Did she listen to music?

✅ Answers (Yes, Subject + Past Verb)

  • Yes, we watched them.
  • Yes, she listened to music.

🧩 Grammar 2: Simple Past (Irregular Verbs)

Some verbs are irregular. They do not take -ed in the past. Instead, their entire word form changes! You need to memorize these special forms.

❓ Questions

  • Did you wear a mask?
  • Did you buy a present?

✅ Answers (Irregular Change)

  • Yes, I wore one. (wear → wore)
  • Yes, I bought her one. (buy → bought)

🟢 Basic Level Exercises

Exercise 1

Complete with regular past form: "We __________ (listen) to music at the party."

Solution: listened.
Explanation: 'Listen' is a regular verb. We add '-ed' to make it past tense.
Exercise 2

Complete the question: "__________ you watch the fireworks?"

Solution: Did.
Explanation: Past simple questions in English always start with the auxiliary verb "Did" for all persons.
Exercise 3

Transform to past: "cook" → __________

Solution: cooked.
Explanation: Regular verb rule: add '-ed' directly to 'cook'.
Exercise 4

Complete: "Yes, I __________ (play) games with my cousins."

Solution: played.
Explanation: Affirmative past tense for regular verb 'play' becomes 'played'.
Exercise 5

Transform to past: "taste" → __________

Solution: tasted.
Explanation: Since 'taste' already ends in 'e', we only add '-d'.

🟡 Intermediate Level Exercises (Irregular Verbs & Sentences)

Exercise 6

Fill in the irregular past form: "Did you wear party hats? Yes, we __________ party hats."

Solution: wore.
Explanation: The irregular past form of 'wear' is 'wore'.
Exercise 7

Match/Complete: "eat" in past tense is __________.

Solution: ate.
Explanation: 'Eat' is an irregular verb that changes to 'ate'.
Exercise 8

Complete: "My friends __________ (sing) a happy birthday song to me."

Solution: sang.
Explanation: Irregular verb 'sing' transforms to 'sang' in the past.
Exercise 9

Complete: "Did you write the invitations? Yes, I __________ the invitations."

Solution: wrote.
Explanation: Irregular verb 'write' becomes 'wrote'.
Exercise 10

Complete: "We __________ (celebrate) my brother's birthday yesterday."

Solution: celebrated.
Explanation: 'Celebrate' is a regular verb ending in 'e', so we add '-d'.

🔴 Advanced Level Exercises (Multi-Verb & Error Correction)

Exercise 11

Correct the sentence: "Did you ate pizza yesterday?"

Solution: "Did you eat pizza yesterday?"
Explanation: When using the auxiliary "Did" in a question, the main verb must return to its base form ("eat", not "ate").
Exercise 12

Fill in both blanks: "After we __________ (dance), we __________ (go) home."

Solution: danced, went.
Explanation: 'Dance' is regular (-ed), while 'go' is irregular (went).
Exercise 13

Translate/Construct: Write a question asking if your friend bought a present.

Solution: "Did you buy a present?"
Explanation: Structure: Did + subject (you) + base verb (buy) + object (a present).
Exercise 14

Fill in: "My grandmother __________ (make) noodle soup and we __________ (taste) it."

Solution: made, tasted.
Explanation: 'Make' is irregular (made), and 'taste' is regular (tasted).
Exercise 15

Spot the mistake and rewrite correctly: "She didn't watched the party."

Solution: "She didn't watch the party."
Explanation: Negative past sentences use "didn't" followed by the base verb ("watch"), not the past tense form.

💭 Critical Reflection & Conceptual Analysis

Discuss these open-ended questions with your teacher or classmates to deepen your understanding.

Question 1: Regular vs. Irregular

Why do you think some verbs change completely in the past (like eat → ate) while others just add -ed? How does knowing both help you tell better stories about your birthday?

Question 2: The Role of "Did"

In questions, we use Did and keep the main verb in its base form. Why is this rule helpful when speaking English quickly with friends?
